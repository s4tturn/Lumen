# Lumen

> **Small things matter.**
>
> Lumen helps you turn small acts of care into visible progress.

Lumen is a wellbeing app built around a simple idea: when life feels overwhelming, you do not always need to fix everything. Sometimes it helps to start with one small thing.

Lumen gives you simple, concrete things to do across six collections: **Self · Space · Kitchen · Connection · Growth · Joy**. When you finish something, your progress becomes part of the world around you.

## Your progress becomes a place

Every completed task adds something to a 3D room. Furniture, objects, and decorations gradually appear as you do more, turning the room into a quiet record of the things you've actually done.

**Do something. See it become real.**

## A different way to navigate

Lumen is built as one continuous space rather than a collection of tabs.

| | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Memory** | A visual history of what you've completed. |
| **Home** | A calm, ambient space of light and motion. |
| **Breathe** | A tactile breathing experience with a responsive fluid form. |
| **Collections** | Six collections of small, meaningful actions. |
| **Room** | A 3D space that grows with your progress. |

From Home, you move between them by swiping in different directions. Navigation is meant to feel more like moving through a place than moving through menus.

## Made for Apple platforms

Lumen is designed from the ground up for iPhone and iPad, with close attention to Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and native platform conventions. Accessibility, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, haptics, gestures, and spatial layout are considered as part of the experience rather than added afterwards.

Liquid Glass is a natural part of the visual language, used alongside motion, depth, typography, and materials to create an interface that feels light and responsive without getting in the way.

## Small, tactile interactions

The interface is meant to respond to you. Press and hold the form in Breathe and it reacts to your touch before settling into a breathing rhythm. Turn the Room and the space moves with you.

Motion throughout the app follows the same restrained feel: fluid when it should be, quiet when it should be, and never there just for decoration.

## The idea behind Lumen

Lumen is not about doing more for the sake of doing more. It is about making small efforts easier to start, a little more rewarding to finish, and easier to remember.

Sometimes one small thing is enough to get moving again.

**Lumen helps you start there.**
